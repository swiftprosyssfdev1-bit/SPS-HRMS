SPS HRMS System Navigation & Features Guide

=== DASHBOARD ===
The Dashboard is the main landing page. It shows your attendance summary, leave balance, and company announcements. Click Dashboard or the company logo in the sidebar to go there.

=== PAYSLIP / PAYROLL ===
To download your monthly salary slip (payslip):
1. Click Payroll in the left sidebar
2. Click My Payslips
3. Find the month you want
4. Click the Download PDF button
The PDF will be saved directly to your computer.

HR Note: To manage payroll for all employees, go to Payroll > Payroll Runs.

=== ATTENDANCE ===
To view your attendance history:
1. Click Attendance in the sidebar
2. Click My Attendance
You can see your daily punch-in and punch-out times and monthly records.

To mark your attendance (punch in/out): Click Attendance > Punch in the sidebar.

Managers/HR: Go to Attendance > Team Monthly Register to view the full team sheet.

=== LEAVES & PERMISSIONS ===
To apply for leave, permission, or WFH (Work From Home):
1. Click Leaves in the sidebar
2. Click My Leaves
3. Click the Apply button
4. Choose the type: Leave, Permission, or WFH
5. Fill in the dates, times, and reason
6. Click Submit - your manager will be notified automatically

To check your leave balance, ask the chatbot: what is my leave balance?

Managers/HR: Go to Leaves > Leave Approvals to approve or reject pending requests.

=== PRODUCTION TRACKER / HOURLY TRACKER ===
The Production Tracker is for employees working on projects with a daily output target.

For Employees - To fill your daily entry:
1. Click Production Tracker in the left sidebar
2. Click Daily Entry
3. Fill in your hourly production numbers
4. Click Save

For Managers/HR - To view team performance dashboard:
- Click Production Tracker > Dashboard
- You can see each employee's daily target vs achievement
- You can set individual targets from the dashboard

To view your past production history: Production Tracker > My Tracker History

=== PROJECTS ===
To view your assigned projects:
- Click Projects > My History in the sidebar

To submit a project progress update:
1. Go to Projects > My History
2. Click on the project name
3. Click Submit Update and fill in the details
4. Submit - your manager will be notified

Managers: Go to Projects > Approvals to review and approve team submissions.

=== EMPLOYEE DIRECTORY ===
To find a colleague's contact info, department, or manager:
- Click Employees > Directory in the sidebar
- Use the search box to find anyone by name or department

=== QUERIES / HELPDESK ===
To raise a question or complaint to HR or your Manager:
1. Click Queries in the sidebar
2. Click My Queries
3. Click New Query
4. Choose who to send it to: HR, Manager, or Admin
5. Type your question or issue
6. Click Submit
HR/Manager will be notified and will reply to your query in the system.

=== APPRAISAL & INCREMENT ===
To view your appraisal history or calculate your expected increment:
- Click Increments > Appraisal Calculator in the sidebar
- Enter your current CTC and performance rating to see an estimate

HR: Go to Increments > All Increments to manage increment requests.

=== LOGOUT ===
To log out: Click Logout at the bottom of the left sidebar.

=== ROLE-BASED SIDEBAR ACCESS ===
Different roles see different items in the sidebar:
- Employees: Dashboard, Attendance (My Attendance, Punch), Leaves (My Leaves), Payroll (My Payslips), Projects (My History), Production Tracker (Daily Entry, My Tracker History), Queries (My Queries)
- Managers: All of the above PLUS Team Attendance, Leave Approvals, Team Queries, Projects Approvals, Production Tracker Dashboard
- HR: All of the above PLUS Payroll Runs, Onboard Employee, Increment Management, Attendance Reports, All Leaves
- COO/Senior Management: Company-wide reports, branch-level data, management dashboards
- Admin: System settings, user management, full access to all modules
